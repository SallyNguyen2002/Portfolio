Sara Keki

The project that started my branding journey.

This was my first attempt at building a complete brand identity from the ground up. Inspired by Japanese cafés and artisan bakeries, I explored how minimal design, soft colors, and thoughtful typography could create a welcoming and memorable dessert brand. While I can now spot the flaws, it's still one of the projects I'm most proud of because it marks the beginning of my design journey.
